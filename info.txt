PLANNER DASHBOARD - OPEN QUESTIONS FOR FUTURE DEVELOPMENT
==========================================================

The dashboard (dashboard.html) works as-is. These questions are about things
I guessed while turning the spreadsheet (reference/todo_framework.xlsx) into
the dashboard. Answer whichever you care about and the next version can be
adjusted to match.


1. DAILIES SHUFFLE
   The spreadsheet shuffles dailies by date: rank 1-9 tasks stay near their
   rank (plus or minus 1.5), and rank 10+ tasks go somewhere random. The
   dashboard uses the same formula.
   - Is the point of the shuffle variety, or something else? Would you rather
     set a fixed order, or have the order follow the clock (morning tasks
     first)?
   - Should optional (rank 10+) tasks be hidden on busy days, for example days
     with a deadline or a work day?
   - Should some dailies only happen on certain weekdays (e.g. gym Mon/Wed/Fri)?

2. "RIGHT NOW" CARD
   In the spreadsheet you moved through the list by changing a number by hand.
   The dashboard shows the first unfinished daily and has Done / Skip buttons.
   - Should "right now" also include deadline work and the planner time slots
     for the current hour, not just dailies?
   - Would a timer for the task's minutes be useful?

3. DEADLINES
   - Do deadlines need a time of day, or is the date enough?
   - Do any deadlines repeat (weekly reports, monthly bills, lessons)? Right
     now each one has to be entered separately.
   - Should a big deadline be breakable into smaller steps/milestones with
     their own dates (e.g. "learn next tense" -> vocab, drills, quiz)?
   - "Due soon" is set to 7 days, "urgent" is 2 days or less. Do those
     thresholds suit you, and should they differ per category (work vs hobby)?
   - What should happen to finished deadlines: keep them forever (hidden
     unless "Show done" is checked), archive them, or delete them after a while?

4. ROTATING WORK SCHEDULE
   The week sheet showed an 8-period cycle that moves forward one period each
   weekday. I assumed that from your example. Set the real starting point in
   Settings.
   - What does the period number mean (a shift, a class period, a team)?
     Should each period have its own label or work hours instead of a number?
   - Do holidays and days off pause the rotation (current behavior) or does
     the rotation keep counting through them?
   - Are work hours the same every day? The sheet showed 8:00 to about 3:30 on
     weekdays.

5. PLANNER
   - The planner starts on today and shows 14 days. Your sheet started on a
     Saturday. Do you want it to always start on a particular weekday?
   - Should planned time slots be able to span several slots (a 2-hour block)
     and be dragged around, instead of labeling 30-minute slots one at a time?
   - Should the "Work on:" hints turn into real planned slots automatically?

6. HOBBIES / CATEGORIES
   The dashboard starts with Work, Language learning and Hobbies.
   - What are your actual hobbies/categories? Should each hobby have its own
     dailies, or is one shared dailies list fine?
   - Would you like to track progress over time (minutes spent per category
     per week, streak history, a chart)?

7. DATA AND DEVICES
   Everything is saved in this browser's local storage, as you asked. That
   means it lives only in one browser on one computer, and clearing browser
   data erases it. (Settings has Download/Restore backup as a safety net.)
   - Do you use more than one computer or your phone? If so, should the data
     sync somehow later, or is one browser enough?
   - Would you like a reminder to download a backup now and then?

8. REMINDERS
   - Would browser notifications help (e.g. "deadline tomorrow", "you haven't
     done your dailies")? They only work while the page is open.
   - Should the dashboard be able to export deadlines to a calendar (.ics file)
     for Apple/Google Calendar?

9. LOOK AND FEEL
   - Is the panel order right (Right now, Deadlines, Dailies, Planner), or is
     something more important than its spot suggests?
   - Keep following your system's light/dark setting, or add a switch?

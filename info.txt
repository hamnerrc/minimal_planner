PLANNER - OPEN QUESTIONS FOR FUTURE DEVELOPMENT
================================================

Design rule: complexity in the backend, simplicity at the point of action.
RN shows one task and a Done button. Configuration holds the knobs. Meta
holds the numbers.

These are the places where I had to guess. Answer the ones you care about
and the next version can follow your answers.


1. RN: WHAT COUNTS AS "NECESSARY TO ACT"?
   RN currently shows the task name, "about N min", any notes, and Done.
   After Done, a small "Undo" link appears for 8 seconds.
   - Keep the duration line and notes, or show the name only?
   - Is the brief Undo link acceptable, or should mistakes be fixed somewhere
     else (e.g. Meta)?
   - Should there be any way to defer a task ("not now")? Right now the only
     way past a task is Done. A skip would mean the backend has to decide
     where the task goes: end of queue, tomorrow, or dropped.
   - When nothing is available, RN says "Nothing right now / Next task at
     6:00 pm" or "Done for today". Is the next-time hint fine, or too much?

2. QUEUE STABILITY VS. EDITS
   Today's queue is locked when the page first opens that day. Mid-day:
   new tasks join the END, deleted or no-longer-available tasks drop out,
   and priority changes wait until tomorrow.
   - Is that right, or should a new priority-1 task jump to the front?
   - Should there be a manual "rebuild today's queue" button in
     Configuration for when you change a lot at once?

3. UNFINISHED TASKS
   At midnight, whatever wasn't done is simply counted as missed (it shows
   in Meta's rates). Repeating tasks come back the next available day.
   One-time tasks stay until done.
   - Should a missed repeating task get a priority boost the next day, or
     carry over?
   - Should one-time tasks that keep getting missed climb in priority?

4. PRIORITY AND RANDOMIZATION
   Kept from the spreadsheet: priority 1-9 shifts by up to ±1.5 per day;
   10+ is optional and placed randomly; seeded by the date and task name.
   - Should some tasks be pinned to a fixed spot (always first)?
   - Should tasks be able to depend on each other (B only after A)?
   - Due dates add 1 rank per day inside the window (default 7 days). Is that
     the right strength?

5. DURATIONS AND THE DAILY BUDGET
   Total budget defaults to 120 min on work days and 240 on days off.
   Priority tasks are always queued even if they exceed it. Optional tasks
   only fill what's left.
   - Should the budget instead be calculated from free time (waking hours
     minus work hours)?
   - Should Meta's measured times automatically correct the estimates?
     (Meta already shows "actual vs. estimate".)

6. AVAILABILITY
   Each task can be limited by weekdays, a time window, "not during work
   hours", rotation periods, a start date, and an end/due date.
   - What does the rotation period number mean in real life (a shift, a
     class period)? Would named periods or per-period work hours help?
   - Do days off pause the rotation (current behavior) or not?
   - Are there other kinds of availability you need: every other day, X
     times per week, monthly, only after a certain other task?
   - One-time tasks with a due date: is Done "finished for good" (current),
     or do some need several sessions before they're finished?

7. META
   It currently tracks tasks done per day, completion rate, done per active
   day, typical time per task, actual vs. estimate, a per-task table, and
   today's log. Time is measured from when a task appears on RN to Done.
   - Is "appeared on screen → Done" a fair measure? If you open RN and walk
     away, the time is inflated (capped at 4 h). A Start button would be more
     accurate but adds a step at the point of action.
   - Are there numbers you want that aren't there: time of day you're most
     productive, streaks, per-category totals?

8. WHAT HAPPENED TO THE FIRST VERSION'S FEATURES
   The first dashboard had a deadline board, a 14-day planner grid with
   time slots, and per-day plan notes. They were left out to fit the
   three-tab design. Deadlines are now one-time tasks with a due date.
   Open deadlines, dailies and the "important" list carry over automatically.
   Events, planner notes and time-slot labels don't.
   - Do you want any of those back, e.g. a calendar view inside Meta or
     Configuration?

9. DATA AND DEVICES
   Local browser storage only, as requested. One browser on one computer;
   clearing browser data erases it (backup download/restore is in
   Configuration).
   - Will you ever want this on your phone too? That would need some form
     of sync, which is a bigger decision.
